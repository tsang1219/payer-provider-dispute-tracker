Key Finding: Humana's Zero Resolution Rate

Of the 49 disputes involving Humana, zero have been resolved. When hospitals threaten to leave Humana's network, the insurer consistently allows them to go out-of-network rather than negotiate. This affects an estimated 1.2 million Medicare Advantage patients across 36 health systems.

Key Insights

Explosive Growth

10x

Disputes increased from 8 in 2021 to 84 in 2024, with 71 already in 2025. The trend shows accelerating conflict between hospitals and insurers.

Medicare Advantage Focus

50+

At least 50 disputes specifically involve Medicare Advantage plans, with hospitals increasingly opting out of MA networks while remaining in-network for commercial plans.

UnitedHealthcare Dominance

52

UnitedHealthcare is involved in 52 disputes (26% of all), affecting an estimated 734,000 patients. They have the highest dispute volume by far.

Q4 Concentration

47

Q4 sees the most disputes (47 in 2024-2025 alone) as contracts typically expire at year-end, creating a seasonal surge in network disruptions.

Resolution Gap

71%

71% of disputes remain unresolved (OON-Ongoing or Unknown status). Once a dispute begins, most do not reach resolution.

Patient Impact

6.5M

Over 6.5 million patients are potentially affected by these disputes, facing higher out-of-pocket costs or needing to find new providers.

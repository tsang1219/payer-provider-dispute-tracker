# Producer Brief — Payer–Provider Dispute Tracker

**Created:** 2026-08-30  
**Format:** Public dataset + interactive tracker + supporting analysis  
**Audience:** Healthcare operators, policy researchers, journalists, and people affected by payer–provider contracting disputes

## Near-term objective

Restore the existing tracker, publish the data and methodology openly, and reintroduce the original analysis. The public value is the assembled longitudinal record; publication should not wait for a false claim of completeness.

## Deliverables

- Reproducible and atomic data-release pipeline.
- Validated canonical CSV and consistent derived artifacts.
- Public GitHub repository with sources, limitations, correction instructions, citation guidance, and versioned releases.
- Deployed interactive tracker.
- A short re-promotion package: updated findings, new visuals, and a “what changed” post pointing back to the original work.

## Maintenance model

- Weekly automated discovery into an append-only candidate queue.
- Monthly status review and reviewed canonical release.
- Automation never approves or silently changes canonical records.
- GitHub pull requests become the audit and correction surface.

## Known limitations to preserve publicly

- Coverage reflects publicly visible reporting and is not comprehensive.
- First-media date is a reporting marker, not necessarily the beginning of negotiations.
- Many patient-impact figures are estimates and must remain visibly labeled.
- Source links may be paywalled, syndicated, or disappear.
- Records can contain errors; corrections and contributed evidence are welcome.

## Later flagship

A standalone Health Affairs article should emerge from a refreshed longitudinal finding—such as dispute duration, recurrence, payer concentration, Medicare Advantage prevalence, permanent separations, or geography. It is not merely an announcement of the database and receives its own research and editorial gate.
